## Asymmetry Gate Assessment

### Criterion 1: Mispriced at live price NOW?

At EV ~$317M with ARR $89.52M, the current valuation is **3.54x ARR**.

The prior §4 scoring used a "3.1x ARR bull ≥2x" framing. If the "bull" was 3.1x ARR, then at $89.52M ARR:
- Bull EV implied = $89.52M × 3.1 = **$277M**
- Current EV = **$317M** — already ABOVE the bull-case EV

The company is trading ABOVE what was calculated as the bull scenario at §4. Even granting C=2 uncertainty of ±15%, the current EV is not clearly cheap:
- At PLN 47.42 and declining profitability: 10.5x P/E on -29% declining EPS = EPS bottoming trend unknown
- Dividend yield 11.7% sounds attractive but payout ratio is 123% of current EPS (unsustainable at current earnings level)
- EV/EBITDA ~8x is not distressed for a near-flat ARR growth company with -28% profitability

→ **Criterion 1: FAIL** — Current price does not represent a clear margin-of-safety mispricing. The stock is trading at FAIR at best, SLIGHTLY RICH at worst for current financials.

### Criterion 2: Realistic bull ≥2x?

From PLN 47.42, a 2x bull = PLN 94.84 → EV ~$634M → requires ~7x ARR at current ARR.

What ARR growth would justify 7x ARR? Typically 30%+ ARR growers. Current ARR growth: +4% YoY.

Even a re-acceleration to 20% ARR CAGR (major acceleration from 4%) over 3 years: ARR = $89.52M × 1.2³ = ~$155M. At 4x ARR = ~$620M EV. That's only 1.95x from current. And 20% CAGR from 4% current is a heroic assumption.

→ **Criterion 2: FAIL** — A genuine 2x bull is not realistic within a 3-year horizon without a dramatic and unconfirmed ARR reacceleration.

### Criterion 3: Upside skewed over downside?

Bull (optimistic): ARR reaccelerates to 15-20%, profitability stabilizes → stock at PLN 70-80 (+47-69%)
Bear: ARR continues at 4%, profitability compressed further, payout cut → stock at PLN 25-35 (-26% to -47%)

→ **Criterion 3: BORDERLINE** — skew is not compelling given the bear scenario is plausible.

### Criterion 4: Discrete catalyst OR CORE-grade quality?

No discrete catalyst: no M&A activity, no new products imminent, next earnings August 28, 2026.

CORE-grade quality requires: >100% NRR OR CORE designation from playbook. NRR not disclosed. GM compression disqualifies CORE.

## Key Risks Summary

1. **NRR opacity**: Cannot distinguish between healthy new-logo growth masking churn vs. genuine retention. Without NRR, the investment case rests on disclosed partial metrics.
2. **GM compression**: From 82% to 68% in ~3 years. Infrastructure cost growth must decelerate or product pricing must increase. If GM falls further, the high-margin SaaS thesis weakens.
3. **Dividend unsustainability**: 123% payout ratio. If EPS continues declining, dividend must be cut. A dividend cut at 11.7% yield = significant stock downside.
4. **Single-product dependency**: 83.7% revenue from LiveChat. ChatBot and HelpDesk are growing but from tiny bases; LiveChat slowdown is the existential risk.
5. **Competition**: Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk — all competing aggressively. LiveChat's positioning as a simpler/cheaper alternative is under pressure.
6. **FX risk**: Revenue in USD, reported in PLN. PLN strengthening = revenue decline even with flat USD growth.
7. **C=2 cap**: Limits promotion regardless of other factors.
