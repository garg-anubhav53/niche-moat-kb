## Company

Markets: 5G PTP/SYNCE synchronization testing (primary; standards-critical conformance testing for IEEE 1588v2 PTP, ITU-T G.8262/G.8264, SYNCE), expanding to cloud/hyperscaler (unnamed hyperscaler repeat order) and US federal/defence. Fiscal year ends 31 March.

## Moat (from Annual Report ✓)
Standards-critical conformance testing for telecom synchronization:
- Products certified against ITU-T G.8262/G.8264, IEEE 1588v2 PTP, SYNCE — the specific standards that telcos MUST prove compliance with for network approval
- "First to market" at each new speed tier (10GbE, 25GbE, 100GbE, 400GbE, 800GbE) — qualification inertia means labs stay with vendor once validated
- ~75% of revenue from repeat customers (AR cited) ~; 3-yr average customer has been with CLX for 10 years ~
- Lab approval process: once test equipment is validated by network operator, switching requires full re-certification → multi-year inertia
- NOT sole-source — competes with VIAVI Solutions, Spirent, Keysight (all much larger); moat is niche positioning + lab-qualification inertia + deep standards expertise
- NEW markets: cloud/hyperscaler (hyperscaler repeat order — 800Gb/s sync testing); US federal/defence (new contracts)
