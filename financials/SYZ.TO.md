## Business

Sylogist Ltd. is a public-sector SaaS ERP provider headquartered in Calgary, Canada. Sells mission-critical ERP, CRM, fundraising/donor management, and education administration software exclusively to:
- Government agencies (municipal, state/provincial, federal)
- Nonprofits and charitable organizations
- Educational institutions (K-12 and higher education)

Over 2,000 customers. Transitioning to "Sylogist One" unified platform with partner-led implementation model (43% of ARR bookings partner-led Q4 2025).
