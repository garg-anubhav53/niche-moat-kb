## Business

UK-listed specialty chemicals in three segments:
- **Personal Care** (~35-40% rev): Rheology modifiers (thickeners/stabilisers) for cosmetics/skin care; hectorite-based compounds are premium tier
- **Coatings** (~30-35% rev): Specialty additives (rheology, anti-settling, corrosion inhibitors) for architectural/industrial paints
- **Talc/Chromium** (~20-30% rev): Natural mineral extraction including the hectorite sole-source mine in Amargosa Valley, Nevada (world's only commercially viable hectorite deposit)

**Moat claim**: Hectorite sole-source — only commercially viable hectorite mine globally; hectorite provides unique swelling/thickening properties that are difficult to replicate synthetically. BUT the moat only covers ~20-30% of group revenues. The Personal Care and Coatings segments face competition from BASF, Ashland, Evonik, Elementis/Huntsman, and specialty Asian producers.
