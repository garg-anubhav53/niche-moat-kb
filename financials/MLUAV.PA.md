## Business

Embention Sistemas Inteligentes SA is a Spanish avionics company listed on Euronext Growth Paris. It designs EASA-certified flight control and avionics systems for UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle), eVTOL (electric Vertical Take-Off and Landing), and autonomous aircraft platforms.

**Certifications (key moat drivers):**
- **EASA POA** (Production Organisation Approval) — authorises production of certified safety-critical avionics; multi-year process to obtain; very few UAV-focused companies hold it
- **EASA APDOA** (Aircraft Parts Design Organisation Approval) — design-authority certification for airborne parts
- **DO-178C** compliance — software for airborne systems (highest safety-critical avionics software standard)
- **DO-254** compliance — design assurance for airborne electronic hardware
- **DO-160G** compliance — environmental conditions and test procedures for airborne equipment

Products include AI-integrated flight controllers, autopilot systems, and certifiable avionics stacks for commercial BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) drone operations, urban air mobility, and autonomous aircraft.

**Revenue model:** Hardware + software licensing for certified avionics stacks. EASA certification qualifies the product for commercial operations in EU airspace — operators purchasing certified avionics avoid 2-4 year self-certification cost.

## Moat Assessment

**REGULATORY_MOAT + CERTIFICATION_MOAT:**
1. EASA POA: Production approval for safety-critical avionics is a genuine 2-4 year, capital-intensive barrier. Operators in EU commercial airspace cannot use non-POA-certified avionics for licensed commercial operations. Embention's competitors (most UAV avionics startups) lack production certification.
2. DO-178C/DO-254 certifiable software: Aerospace-qualified software/hardware is a distinct technical category from non-certified drone autopilots (e.g. ArduPilot, PX4 open-source). Customer lock-in: once an airframe is certified with Embention avionics embedded, recertification cost of switching is substantial.
3. BVLOS regulatory expansion: European EASA UAS regulation (EU 2019/945 + amendments) is progressively opening commercial BVLOS operations, creating growing demand for certified avionics.
4. 49% EBITDA margin ~ implies software-heavy IP licensing model — consistent with certification-based pricing power.
