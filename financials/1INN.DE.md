## Key Risks
1. **BINARY REGULATORY RISK (primary):** Entire TAM depends on German Forschungslagengesetz (FZulG) R&D tax incentive framework. If Germany eliminates or materially restructures BSFZ certification → revenue could collapse. This is the sole material existential risk. Risk mitigant: German R&D policy has bipartisan support; Scholz government (SPD+CDU/CSU) historically supportive; but political risk is real.
2. **IPO overhang:** Stock is 40% below €120 IPO price at ~€72.50. Insiders locked for 6/12/24 months from May 2025 IPO; if lock-ups have expired (May 2026 = 12 months), insider selling pressure possible. Free float only 18.2% = any selling is amplified.
3. **Scale-up risk:** Expansion to France/UK requires regulatory framework differences; BSFZ applies only in Germany; France has CIR (Crédit d'impôt recherche) and UK has R&D tax credits — different systems requiring adaptation of Clusterix.
4. **Competitive entry:** At 61% EBIT margins, profitable moat attracts competitors. Moat durability relies on Clusterix's regulatory certification relationships + data network effects.
5. **Illiquidity:** Only ~18.2% free float on Frankfurt Scale = thin daily trading volume; institutional position sizing constrained.
