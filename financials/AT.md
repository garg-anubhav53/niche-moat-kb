## Moat
Subsea equipment rental with asset integrity NDT:
- Specialist equipment (deepwater ROVs, inspection vehicles, NDT tools) requires operator certification and project-specific deployment qualification
- Long-term frame agreements with major offshore operators (BP, Shell, Equinor, Petrobras, Saudi Aramco etc.) — incumbency advantage
- Asset Integrity NDT: regulatory inspection cycle (mandatory periodic structural inspection of offshore assets under HSE/IMO/DNV-GL rules) → captive recurring demand
- NOT sole-source — competes with Oceaneering International, Saipem, DOF Subsea, Reach Subsea; moat is specialist certification + frame-agreement incumbency, not monopoly
- Organic growth only ~3% — franchise somewhat acquisition-dependent for revenue growth
