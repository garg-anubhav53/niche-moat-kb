## Key Risks
- **Export license refusals**: >€8M in customer orders have been refused by French export control authorities. Riber's MBE equipment is dual-use (compound semiconductor chambers); refusals likely target orders to controlled jurisdictions (China probable). This is a material constraint on the most high-demand customer geography.
- **Backlog quality**: €26.9M backlog must be monitored for license-uncertain orders. Further refusals would impair backlog conversion rate and NI growth trajectory.
