## Moat Assessment
- **UK niche dominance**: Only scaled egg-free cake franchise chain (251 stores vs ~24 for nearest competitor = ~10x scale). 17-year brand equity in South Asian and halal communities. Growing demographic tailwind.
- **Supply-chain lock-in**: Franchisees source ~45% of revenues back to Cake Box HQ as mandatory ingredient purchases (sponge, cream, packaging). Switching would require: alternative egg-free supply chains, rebranding, and sacrificing brand recognition vs. £125-150K sunk franchisee investment.
- **Asset-light model**: Franchise capex borne by franchisees; HQ capex minimal (£3.1M vs £42.78M revenue = 7.3%).
- **Moat rating**: NARROW — real but could be eroded by a well-capitalised competitor. Has not happened in 17 years, suggesting economics do not attract large players (market niche is large enough for Cake Box but small enough to be unattractive to major bakery chains).
