## Moat Assessment
**Type:** SWITCHING COST / VMS SERIAL ACQUIRER — Nordic healthcare ancillary clinical software

**Structure:** Omda is a Constellation Software / Topicus-style VMS serial acquirer, acquiring niche clinical software verticals with high switching cost:
- **Blood management (ProSang):** 100% market share in Sweden + Denmark for hospital blood management (transfusion medicine workflow); only product in its class in these markets; re-implementation requires multi-year data migration + regulatory validation
- **Oncology dosing (Cytodose):** Chemotherapy dosing decision support with pharmacovigilance integration; mission-critical patient safety function; clinical workflow embedded
- **Cardiac ECG (Cardio):** ECG system interoperability for Nordic hospital networks; spec-in device integration
- **Emergency dispatch/CAD (Predicare/Aweria):** Computer-aided dispatch for Nordic emergency services; public safety software with long procurement cycles and high public-sector switching cost
- **Saab Public Safety Solutions (June 2026):** Saab AB's public safety dispatch software for UK/Ireland/Sweden; extends the emergency dispatch footprint to UK
- **Teledermatology (Dermicus):** AI-assisted dermatology triage platform; clinical decision support in primary care

**Moat strength:** The niche-within-niche structure is consistent with high durability — each product is mission-critical in a small clinical domain; no single competitor spans all verticals. Nordic public health procurement cycles are long (5-10 years) and regulatory validation re-runs are expensive. Network effects modest (not strong) — value is switching cost, not network.

**Key moat risk:** Individual subsidiaries are small-scale; organic growth of the underlying businesses is modest; the business model requires continuous acquisition to maintain revenue growth trajectory. If acquisition pipeline dries or integration quality declines, organic growth alone (~3-5%) would not support current valuation multiples.
