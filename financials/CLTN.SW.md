## Business

COLTENE Holding AG is a Swiss manufacturer and distributor of dental consumables and small dental equipment. Three product groups:
1. **Dental Improvement** (~45% est. rev): Composites (conventional, bulk-fill, CAD/CAM, core build-up), bondings, luting cements, etching gels, curing lights
2. **Treatment Auxiliaries** (~35% est. rev): Endodontic instruments (NiTi rotary/reciprocating files, endo motors), impression materials
3. **Infection Control** (~20% est. rev): Sterilization, hygiene, infection prevention consumables

**Distribution**: Own sales orgs in Germany, Switzerland, USA, Canada, Brazil, Japan, China, India; supplemented by distributor networks in 100+ countries. Sells B2B to dental practices, DSOs, dental labs.

**Moat thesis**: Dental school specification — practitioners trained on specific brands for 5-6 years maintain brand habits throughout their career. CE/FDA registration per product per device type creates regulatory barriers to substitution. Multi-channel distribution with global footprint. HOWEVER: Mid-tier position (#3-4 globally); direct competition from Tier-1 (3M ESPE, Dentsply Sirona, Ivoclar Vivadent, GC Corporation, Kerr — ~52-55% global market share collectively). No sole-source raw material, no regulatory monopoly.
