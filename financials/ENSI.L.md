## Business

EnSilica Plc is a UK fabless ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) designer specialising in satellite communications chips and mixed-signal ASICs for defence, automotive, and industrial markets. HQ: Abingdon, Oxfordshire, UK.

Key product: **ENS92040** — distributed digital beamformer chip for LEO (Low Earth Orbit) satellite user terminals. Multi-beam phased array beamforming chip enabling LEO ground terminal antenna to track multiple satellites simultaneously. Developed co-operatively with ESA (European Space Agency) and UKSA (UK Space Agency) grant funding.

Also designs custom ASICs for defence electronics, industrial control, and automotive applications (AI chips, motor controllers, comms chips).

**Revenue model:** Primarily design services (NRE: Non-Recurring Engineering fees) + chip royalties/production. Fabless = no fab COGS; wafer produced at TSMC/GlobalFoundries.
