## Business Description

Text SA provides B2B SaaS customer communication tools globally. Products by FY2025/26 revenue share:
- **LiveChat** (live chat for websites): 83.7% of revenue
- **ChatBot**: 8.7% / PLN 28.7M (+9.7% YoY)
- **HelpDesk**: 7.6% / PLN 24.9M (+81.7% YoY — from small base)
- **KnowledgeBase, OpenWidget**: minor

Customers: 28,000+ paid in 150+ countries (Adobe, PayPal, Best Buy cited). ~40% of MRR from multi-product customers (up ~5pp YoY). High-value customers (>$500/mo): >55% of MRR (up from ~45% YoY) — deliberate upmarket migration underway.

Revenue is virtually 100% USD-denominated, reported in PLN. FY2025/26 average USD/PLN rate ~3.73 (vs ~4.00 prior year) — FX headwind caused PLN revenue decline despite near-flat USD revenue.
