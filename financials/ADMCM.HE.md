## Moat Assessment
**Type:** SWITCHING COST — dominant cloud-native construction ERP for Finnish SMEs

- **Mission-critical functions:** Payroll and invoicing are legally required; once embedded, re-implementation requires multi-month data migration + retraining
- **Market position:** Dominant cloud-native construction ERP in Finland (15+ year operating history; ~30,000 construction companies in Finland addressable)
- **Competitive moat:** Legacy competitors are on-premise (not cloud-native); Admicom's cloud-native architecture = material product advantage; switching to legacy = backwards migration
- **NRR >106%:** Net expansion via upsell of new modules (quality management, procurement) to existing base
- **Churn <6%:** Implies average customer retention >16 years; no competitor with comparable market penetration
- **Geographic scope:** Finland (core, dominant) + early Estonia (emerging)

**Moat risk:** Finnish construction is geographically narrow (Finland only at material scale). A prolonged construction downturn triggers customer bankruptcies = involuntary churn. ARR declined Q1 2026 due to this exact mechanism.
