## Business Description

Quartix Technologies plc provides vehicle telematics and fleet management SaaS subscriptions. Hardware (GPS tracker) is sold/installed at cost; recurring subscription revenue (~£8–10/vehicle/month) is the economic engine. Customers are predominantly UK SME fleets and insurance telematics providers. As of FY2025 the business serves 333,922 subscribed vehicles (+11% YoY), across ~9% more customers. The company also operates in France and the US (smaller). Moat: high switching costs (installed hardware + fleet workflow dependency), low churn (NRR 98.1% = 1.9% net annual churn), and proprietary 4G TCSV 17 hardware platform launched H2 2025.

**AIM listed:** London Stock Exchange AIM. ISIN: GB00B080FP75. FY end: 31 December.
