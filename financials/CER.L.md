## Business / Moat Summary (§2B Provisional)
**What Cerillion does:** BSS/OSS billing and customer management platform for telecoms operators. Core product suite (Cerillion Skyline): end-to-end SaaS billing system covering mediation, rating, charging, CRM, order management, policy, and finance integrations for mobile/broadband/cable operators.

**Why the moat is real:**
- **Mission-critical integration:** Billing = the revenue engine of a telecom operator. Migration risk is existential (a billing migration failure = customer billing outage = regulatory breach). Switching cost is multi-year.
- **Multi-year contracts:** Cerillion contracts are typically 5-10 year SaaS agreements. Revenue recognition is milestone/delivery-based, not fully ratable, creating the near-term headwind observed.
- **Long requalification cycles:** Replacement BSS/OSS projects take 18-36 months minimum to scope, procure, and deliver. Installed operators face enormous switching friction.
- **Niche specialist:** Cerillion is not SAP or Oracle — it targets mid-size and challenger telecoms globally (not the largest operators). This niche positioning gives it contract wins where larger vendors are overkill.
- **Record backlog acceleration:** £82.1M backlog (up 44% from £56.9M FY2025) + orders doubled in H1 FY2026 = structural demand accelerating, not decelerating.

**Competitive position:** Competes with Amdocs (CAP_KILL huge), CSG Systems, Netcracker (NEC subsidiary), TEOCO, and Comarch. Cerillion's positioning in the mid-market tier with full-stack capability is differentiated.
