## Business / Moat Summary
**What Cerillion does:** BSS/OSS billing and customer management platform for telecoms operators. Core product suite (Cerillion Skyline): end-to-end billing system covering mediation, rating, charging, CRM, order management, policy, and finance integrations for mobile/broadband/cable operators. Sold to mid-size and challenger telecoms globally.

**Why the moat is real:**
- **Mission-critical integration:** Billing = the revenue engine of a telecom operator. A billing migration failure = customer billing outage = regulatory breach. Switching cost is multi-year.
- **Long requalification cycles:** Replacement BSS/OSS projects take 18-36 months to scope, procure, and deliver. Installed operators face enormous switching friction.
- **Niche specialist:** Not SAP or Oracle — targets mid-size and challenger telecoms globally where larger vendors are overkill. This niche positioning enables wins competitors cannot easily replicate.
- **Record bookings:** Orders doubled in H1 FY2026; largest contract in company history; backlog £82.1M (+64%) = structural demand accelerating.

**Moat limit:** 35% truly recurring (not 100%); revenue is lumpy project-based; concentrated on a single product category (billing/BSS). No proprietary data or network effects — pure switching cost moat. If a major customer churns post-contract, the impact is asymmetric.

**Competitive position:** Competes with Amdocs (cap_kill), CSG Systems, Netcracker (NEC subsidiary), TEOCO, Comarch. Mid-market niche differentiated.
